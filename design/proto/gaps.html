What counts

Only real gaps make the list.

A question counts only if nothing in the knowledge base answers it, not if the person asking just isn't cleared for the document that does.

Answered

“When does the wifi password change?”

Company handbook, p. 11 answers it. Not a gap.

Not cleared

“How do I roll back a release?”

The Deploy runbook answers it, but this person can't read it. Not a gap, and the asker isn't told the document exists.

Gap

“What is the parking policy?”

Nothing in any document covers it. Counted, ranked, and waiting for someone to write it.
