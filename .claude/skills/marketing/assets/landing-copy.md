## Subhead
Private. Multi-tenant. Citations on every answer. Set up in 5 minutes —
no ML team, no config.

## Three value props
1. **Ask, get cited answers.** Upload PDF/Word. Ask in plain English.
   Every answer points to the source doc and page.
2. **Knowledge Gap Detection.** The feature ChatGPT can't give you:
   a ranked list of what your team keeps asking that your docs don't
   answer. Know which doc to write next.
3. **Yours, private, cheap.** Per-tenant isolation on your own cloud.
   ~10x lower cost than enterprise RAG — so the free tier is real.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. Upload your docs (PDF, .docx).
2. Your team asks questions, gets cited answers.
3. Open the Gaps dashboard — see your knowledge blind spots, ranked.
