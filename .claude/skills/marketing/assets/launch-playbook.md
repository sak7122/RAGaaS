# Launch playbook (organic channels — no spend, no spam)

Order: build proof → soft launch to warm circles → public launch.
App: https://snappy-mapper-498223-b2.web.app

## 0. Pre-launch (1 week)
- Seed a public demo workspace with sample docs (already have
  scripts/fetch_sample_pdfs.py output).
- Record a 90s Loom: upload → cited answer → Gaps dashboard.
- Get 1–3 design partners (people you know with messy docs). Their gap
  screenshot = your only real social proof. Earn it, don't fake it.

## 1. LinkedIn (your network = warmest, compliant)
- Founder post: the *insight*, not the product. "I built a thing that
  tells support teams what their help center fails to answer. Here's the
  top-10 gap report from a real team." Screenshot. Link in comments.
- DM only people you actually know or have a real reason to. 1:1, not
  copy-paste.

## 2. Product Hunt
- Tuesday–Thursday launch. Title = the differentiator, not "RAG tool."
  e.g. "RAGaaS — ask your docs, and see what they can't answer."
- First comment: the problem story + Loom. Reply to every comment.
- Line up warm upvoters the day before (people who already saw the demo).

## 3. Reddit / communities (give value, don't shill)
- r/smallbusiness, r/CustomerSuccess, r/msp, indie hacker forums.
- Post the *gap-report idea* as a discussion ("how do you find what your
  docs don't cover?"), mention the tool only if asked / in comments.
- Read each sub's self-promo rules first. Getting banned kills the channel.

## 4. Indie / SaaS directories
- BetaList, indie launch lists, "I made this" threads. Low effort, slow
  trickle of opt-in signups → feed into leads.csv.

## 5. Content (compounding)
- One post: "What your help center can't answer (and how to find out)."
  This is the gap-detection idea as a thought-leadership piece. SEO +
  shareable.

## Metrics to track (in leads.csv + a simple tally)
- Demo-workspace visits → signups → docs uploaded → questions asked →
  gap-dashboard opens → demo booked → won.
- The activation moment is **opening the Gaps dashboard.** Drive to it.

## What NOT to do
- No buying email lists. No mass cold DMs. No fake reviews/upvotes.
- No "growth hack" that scrapes strangers. It burns the domain and the
  brand for a week of vanity numbers.
